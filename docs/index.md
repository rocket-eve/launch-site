# Launch Updates ![NASA 36.406 mission patch](img/eve-36406-patch.png){ height="72" style="vertical-align: middle; margin-left: 0.4em;" }

The latest updates on the status of the NASA 36.406 solar EUV calibration rocket launch will be shared below. The launch is scheduled for December 11, 2026.

### 20 November 2026

All of the rocket subsystems for NASA 36.406 have been integrated and we're preparing to do sequence tests in preparation for environmental tests next week.

### 18 November 2026

The team is at the White Sands Missile Range and has begun preparations for the 36.406 sounding rocket launch.
