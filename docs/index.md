### 25 November 2026

Today was Spin Balance and Mass Properties. Had to use some pretty sizeable lead bricks to balance the payload out, but the team got there!

### 24 November 2026

Post-vibe sequence test went well! Built back up for a successful bend test in the evening.

### 23 November 2026

The rocket went through vibe today. Sequence testing tomorrow but for now all systems appear functional!

### 20 November 2026

All of the rocket subsystems for NASA 36.406 have been integrated, sequence tests completed with minimal notes, GPS rollout completed, payload is back in I-lab and will be pumping down through the weekend in preparation for environmental tests next week.
